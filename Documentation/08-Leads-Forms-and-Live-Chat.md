# Leads, Forms & Live Chat

These live under **Operations** in the sidebar — this is where enquiries from the public website actually land.

## Form Submissions

Every form filled out on the public site shows up here: **Quick Enquiry** (the hero section's short form), **Request a Callback** (the contact page), and **Map Access Request** (a visitor unlocking a property's plot map by leaving their details).

- Filter by which form or by status (**New**, **Contacted**, **Closed**) using the dropdowns at the top.
- Click any row to open the full details — name, phone, email, and whatever message/notes they left.
- Update the status as you follow up, so the team can see at a glance what's outstanding.
- **Export** downloads the current filtered list as a CSV (e.g. to open in Excel) for external use.

## Live Chat (CRM)

The **CRM** page is the inbox for the public site's live chat widget.

- A green dot next to a conversation means it has an unread message.
- Status is **Open** or **Closed** — mark a conversation closed once it's resolved.
- Click a conversation to open the full thread and reply.
- **Auto-Reply Settings** lets you turn on an automatic first message a visitor sees the moment they open the chat (e.g. "Thanks for reaching out — a team member will respond shortly"), and edit its text.
