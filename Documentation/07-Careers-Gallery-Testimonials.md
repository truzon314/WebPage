# Careers, Gallery & Testimonials

Three simpler content sections, each managed the same way: a list page with a **+ New** button that opens a side panel (drawer) to fill in and save. None of these have the rich block-editor or SEO panel that Properties/Blog/Pages have — they're intentionally simple.

## Careers

Job postings shown on the public Careers page.

- **Title, Department, Location, Employment type** — the job basics.
- **Description** — the full posting text.
- **Apply email** — where the public site's "Apply" button sends applicants (opens their email app addressed to this).
- **Published** checkbox — unpublished postings don't show on the site.

## Gallery

Photos shown on the public Gallery page.

- **Image** — pick or upload from the Media Library.
- **Caption** — short text shown under the photo.
- **Category** — a free-text label used for grouping/filtering on the public gallery.
- **Sort order** — a number; lower numbers show first.
- **Published** checkbox.

## Testimonials

Customer quotes — shown both on the dedicated Testimonials page and, when marked **Featured**, in the rotating testimonials section on the Home page.

- **Name, Role/Location** — who's being quoted (e.g. "Priya Sharma" / "Banjara Hills").
- **Quote** — the testimonial text.
- **Photo** (optional).
- **Rating** — an optional star rating.
- **Featured** checkbox — controls whether this one appears in the homepage carousel.
- **Published** checkbox — controls whether it shows on the site at all.

## Deleting

Each drawer has a **Delete** button when editing an existing item. Unlike Properties/Blog/Pages/Media, these three don't go through Trash — deleting here is immediate and permanent, so double-check before confirming.
