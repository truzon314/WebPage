# Blog Posts

**Blog Posts** in the sidebar manages the articles shown on the public site's Blog section.

## Creating a post

1. **Blog Posts** → **+ New Post**.
2. Give it a title — this opens the full post editor.

## Writing

The main editor area is a rich-text content editor for the article body. To the side, the **post details panel** covers:

- **Featured image** — the cover photo shown on the blog list and at the top of the article (recommended 1200×675px, 16:9).
- **Reading time** — leave blank and it's calculated automatically from the word count, or set a fixed number of minutes.
- **Categories** and **Tags** — checkboxes; used for the blog's own filtering on the public site.

## Status: Draft, Scheduled, Published

Every post is one of three states:

- **Draft** — not visible to the public, still being worked on.
- **Scheduled** — set to go live automatically at a future date/time you pick.
- **Published** — live on the site right now.

Use **Publish** to make a draft go live immediately, or **Schedule** to set a future publish time instead.

## Version history

Every save is kept as a version. If a post gets messed up, open its version history and restore an earlier one — this replaces the current content and title, so use it carefully.

## Preview

Before publishing, use **Preview** to see exactly how the post will look on the live site without actually publishing it yet.

## SEO

Same as properties — a per-post **SEO & Social Metadata** panel at the bottom controls the search-result and social-share appearance. Leave it blank to fall back to the post's title/excerpt/featured image automatically.
