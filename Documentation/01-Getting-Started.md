# Getting Started

## Logging in

Go to the CMS admin address and sign in with the email and password given to you. Both fields are required.

If you can't remember your password: click **Forgot password?** on the login screen, enter your email, and check your inbox for a reset link. For this to actually work, the site's outgoing email (SMTP) needs to be configured — see [12 — Settings](12-Settings.md#smtp-email) if resets aren't arriving.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left is split into three groups:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **CMS** | Everything content-related — Dashboard, Pages, Blog Posts, Projects, Map Management, Categories, Tags, Media Library, Careers, Gallery, Testimonials, SEO Module, Menus |
| **Operations** | Day-to-day business tools — Form Submissions, CRM (live chat) |
| **System** | Admin-only housekeeping — Users, Roles, Trash, Audit Logs, Settings |

Both **CMS** and **Operations** have a small arrow next to the section heading — click it to collapse that group if you want more room, click again to expand it.

You'll only see the sections your role actually has access to. If something listed in this documentation is missing from your sidebar, that's expected — it means your account's role doesn't include it. See [11 — Users, Roles & Permissions](11-Users-Roles-and-Permissions.md) if you think that's a mistake.

## The Dashboard

The first thing you see after logging in — a quick overview, not something you edit. Use the sidebar to get to the section you actually need.

## Signing out

Click your name/avatar in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, then **Sign out**.
