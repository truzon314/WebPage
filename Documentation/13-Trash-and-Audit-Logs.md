# Trash & Audit Logs

Two safety-net tools under **System**.

## Trash

When you delete a **Blog Post**, **Property**, or **Media** file, it doesn't disappear immediately — it moves here first. Open **Trash**, find the item, and click **Restore** to bring it back exactly as it was.

Only those three types go through Trash. **Careers, Gallery, Testimonials, and Pages** don't have a trash safety net — deleting those is immediate, so double-check before confirming on those specifically. (Users currently have no delete option at all — see [11 — Users, Roles & Permissions](11-Users-Roles-and-Permissions.md#removing-access).)

Trash doesn't empty itself — items sit there until someone permanently removes them.

## Audit Logs

A running history of every change made in the CMS: who did it, what action, what it was done to, and when. Use this to answer "who published that?" or "when did this property's price change?" without having to ask around.

This is read-only and can't be edited or deleted by anyone — it's the one place in the CMS designed to always tell the truth about what happened.
